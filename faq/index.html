Is a valid tax invoice enough to recover input VAT in the UAE?

Not since 1 October 2026. If the FTA establishes evasion in your supply chain, you also need to show you ran the Decision No. 13 checks and kept the evidence.

Full guideYour Tax Invoice Won't Save Your Input VAT Anymore →
Are the Decision No. 13 checks mandatory for every purchase?

No. VATP048 says they don't have to be applied to every supply. They matter where the FTA establishes evasion, and you can't know in advance which supplier that will be.

Full guideYour Tax Invoice Won't Save Your Input VAT Anymore →
Who has to prove what under Decision No. 13?

The FTA must prove tax evasion. Once it does, you must prove the verification checks you ran.

Full guideYour Tax Invoice Won't Save Your Input VAT Anymore →
What supplier checks does FTA Decision No. 13 require?

Identity, a real place of business that fits the activity, and three risk indicators. Over AED 375,000 of spend you also need a bank account letter and a reputation review.

Full guideWho Are You Actually Buying From? The Supplier Checks the FTA Now Expects →
Is a red flag an automatic reason to stop buying from a supplier?

No. A flag needs a clear, documented explanation consistent with your evidence. Proceeding without one is the problem.

Full guideWho Are You Actually Buying From? The Supplier Checks the FTA Now Expects →
Is checking the supplier's TRN enough?

No. The FTA expects identity, place of business and risk checks as well, all documented before the first VAT-able supply.

Full guideWho Are You Actually Buying From? The Supplier Checks the FTA Now Expects →
Can I skip supplier checks on invoices under AED 10,000?

Only if total spend with that supplier doesn't exceed AED 100,000 in the past 12 months or the next 12 months. The test is per supplier, not per invoice.

Full guideAED 10K, 100K, 375K: The Three Numbers That Decide How Deep You Verify Suppliers →
Does splitting invoices avoid the verification thresholds?

No. The thresholds look at cumulative spend per supplier, backward and forward, including known contract values.

Full guideAED 10K, 100K, 375K: The Three Numbers That Decide How Deep You Verify Suppliers →
How often must suppliers be re-verified?

Before the first supply, for each taxable supply, and again for recurring suppliers not checked in the past 12 months.

Full guideAED 10K, 100K, 375K: The Three Numbers That Decide How Deep You Verify Suppliers →
Can I pay a UAE supplier's invoice into a foreign or third-party bank account?

Only with a reasonable commercial explanation that fits your evidence. In FTA Example 4, accepting 'convenience' as the reason meant the buyer should have been aware.

Full guideToo Cheap, Wrong Licence, Odd Bank Account: 4 Red Flags That Can Cost You Input VAT →
Is a very low price a VAT red flag?

Yes. In FTA Example 5, a hotel paid half the bottom of the market range without checking, and should have been aware. A real discount needs a written reason before you buy.

Full guideToo Cheap, Wrong Licence, Odd Bank Account: 4 Red Flags That Can Cost You Input VAT →
Why check the supplier's trade licence?

The goods or services must fall within the supplier's licensed activity. In FTA Example 6, a licence for consulting and IT only showed the supplier couldn't sell shishas.

Full guideToo Cheap, Wrong Licence, Odd Bank Account: 4 Red Flags That Can Cost You Input VAT →
What does FTA Example 8 show?

A business that applied all the Decision No. 13 checks and kept the evidence was treated as unaware of evasion elsewhere in its chain, so its input VAT stood.

Full guideIf It Isn't Documented, It Didn't Happen: Protecting Your Input VAT Under Decision No. 13 →
Do I need a written supplier verification policy?

Yes. Article 5(4) expects a written policy naming who implements, reviews and supervises the checks, kept where tax records are kept.

Full guideIf It Isn't Documented, It Didn't Happen: Protecting Your Input VAT Under Decision No. 13 →
What goes in a supplier evidence file?

ID and licence copies, place of business proof, the risk review, a bank letter and reputation review above AED 375,000, and the supply assessment of price, scope, origin and payment.

Full guideIf It Isn't Documented, It Didn't Happen: Protecting Your Input VAT Under Decision No. 13 →
Do vending machine sales need a tax invoice in the UAE?

No. Under FTA Directive No. 8 of 2026, a Taxable Person selling goods or services through a vending machine doesn't have to issue a tax invoice, provided it keeps sufficient records of the supplies.

Full guideVending Machines and UAE VAT: No Tax Invoice Needed, but the Records and Dates Still Matter →
What records are needed for vending machine sales?

At a minimum: a description of the goods or services supplied, the total consideration received and VAT charged, and the date of supply under Article 26(2) of the VAT Law.

Full guideVending Machines and UAE VAT: No Tax Invoice Needed, but the Records and Dates Still Matter →
What counts as a vending machine for UAE VAT?

An automated self-service device that supplies goods or services automatically and immediately when the customer physically interacts with it, with no human intervention by the supplier. Running, maintaining, monitoring or restocking the machine doesn't count as human intervention.

Full guideVending Machines and UAE VAT: No Tax Invoice Needed, but the Records and Dates Still Matter →
When is VAT due if someone else operates my vending machine?

The consideration is treated as collected on the earlier of the date the operator confirms the collection and the amount, or the day after 30 days from the date the operator collected it.

Corporate TaxAll Corporate Tax guides →
Is a Corporate Tax Group the same as a VAT group?

No. A Corporate Tax Group is a separate regime under Article 40 of the Corporate Tax Law. Having a VAT group doesn't give you a Corporate Tax Group, or the other way round.

Full guideOne Return for the Whole Group, and One Shared Tax Bill: UAE Corporate Tax Groups Explained →
Does each company in a Tax Group get its own 0% band?

No. The group gets one 0% band on the first AED 375,000 of its combined Taxable Income, however many members it has.

Full guideOne Return for the Whole Group, and One Shared Tax Bill: UAE Corporate Tax Groups Explained →
Who is liable for a Tax Group's Corporate Tax?

Every member is jointly and severally liable for the group's Corporate Tax for the periods it was a member, even after it leaves. Administrative penalties stay with the Parent Company, and the group can apply to the FTA to limit joint liability.

Full guideOne Return for the Whole Group, and One Shared Tax Bill: UAE Corporate Tax Groups Explained →
How is indirect ownership counted for the 95% test?

Percentages multiply down the chain, and only through companies that are themselves in the group. If A owns 95% of B and B owns 95% of C, A's stake in C is 90.25%, so C can't join A's group.

Full guideThe 95% Test: Can Your Companies Form a UAE Tax Group? →
Can a foreign parent company be in a UAE Tax Group?

No. A non-resident company can't be a member. Its UAE subsidiaries can only group under a UAE resident Parent Company.

Full guideThe 95% Test: Can Your Companies Form a UAE Tax Group? →
Does the 95% test use the number of shares?

No. It uses legal ownership measured by nominal value. Holding 99% of shares by count can be only 50% by nominal value, which fails the test.

Full guideThe 95% Test: Can Your Companies Form a UAE Tax Group? →
What happens if a Tax Group member drops below 95% for a few days?

It is treated as outside the group for the whole Tax Period. In FTA Example 12, seven days at 90% took the subsidiary out from 1 January, so it had to file its own return for the year.

Full guideJoining, Leaving or Closing a UAE Tax Group: The Timing Rules That Catch Groups Out →
When does a sold subsidiary leave the Tax Group?

From the start of the Tax Period in which the sale happens, along with any subsidiaries below it. The buyer can't group with it for that period either.

Full guideJoining, Leaving or Closing a UAE Tax Group: The Timing Rules That Catch Groups Out →
Does a company deregister when it leaves a Tax Group?

No. It keeps its own TRN and files standalone returns. It stays jointly liable for the group's tax for the years it was a member.

Full guideJoining, Leaving or Closing a UAE Tax Group: The Timing Rules That Catch Groups Out →
Are Aggregated Financial Statements the same as consolidated accounts?

No. Only Tax Group members are included, goodwill and fair value uplifts from buying a company are left out, and the Parent's investment and the subsidiary's equity are both kept.

Full guideAggregated Financial Statements: How a UAE Tax Group's Taxable Income Is Built →
Do Aggregated Financial Statements need to be audited?

Yes. They need a special purpose audit and must be submitted to the FTA within 9 months of the year end. The members' own statements don't need auditing for Corporate Tax purposes.

Full guideAggregated Financial Statements: How a UAE Tax Group's Taxable Income Is Built →
Is an impairment of a loan to another group member deductible?

No. An impairment between members is eliminated on aggregation, so it doesn't reduce the group's tax (FTA Example 18).

Full guideAggregated Financial Statements: How a UAE Tax Group's Taxable Income Is Built →
When is a gain on an intra-group asset transfer clawed back?

When either company leaves the Tax Group within 2 years of the transfer. The gain is measured at market value at the transfer date and taxed in the group on the date the company leaves.

Full guideThe 2-Year Clawback: Moving Assets Inside a UAE Tax Group →
Can relief switch off the 2-year clawback?

Yes, if the gain would have been exempt anyway under Qualifying Group Relief, Business Restructuring Relief or the Participation Exemption, and that relief wouldn't itself have been clawed back.

Full guideThe 2-Year Clawback: Moving Assets Inside a UAE Tax Group →
What tax value does an asset have when its owner leaves the group?

The value in the group's Aggregated Financial Statements. If the clawback applies, the tax cost steps up to the market value at the transfer date.

Full guideThe 2-Year Clawback: Moving Assets Inside a UAE Tax Group →
Can a Tax Group use a new member's losses from before it joined?

Only against Taxable Income attributable to that member, worked out as if it filed alone, and subject to the 75% cap on the group's Taxable Income.

Full guideLosses, Interest and Reliefs in a UAE Tax Group: What's Shared and What's Ring-Fenced →
Can pre-Grouping losses be forfeited?

Yes. If the group has room to use a member's pre-Grouping loss but doesn't attribute income to that member, the whole loss is forfeited (FTA Example 37).

Full guideLosses, Interest and Reliefs in a UAE Tax Group: What's Shared and What's Ring-Fenced →
Can a Tax Group get Small Business Relief?

Only if the group's combined revenue is AED 3 million or less in the current and every previous Tax Period, and no member belongs to a multinational group that files Country-by-Country Reports.

Full guideLosses, Interest and Reliefs in a UAE Tax Group: What's Shared and What's Ring-Fenced →
Is every UAE free zone a Designated Zone?

No. A free zone is only a VAT Designated Zone if it is listed in Cabinet Decision No. 59 of 2017 and meets the conditions in Article 51(1) of the VAT Executive Regulation. Corporate Tax and Excise Tax have their own separate tests.

Full guideFree Zone or Designated Zone? Your Address Has to Pass Four Different Tax Tests →
Is a VAT Designated Zone automatically a Designated Zone for Corporate Tax?

No. TAXP010 says the zone must be both a VAT Designated Zone and a Free Zone for Corporate Tax purposes. Confirm it with your free zone authority.

Full guideFree Zone or Designated Zone? Your Address Has to Pass Four Different Tax Tests →
Can a Qualifying Free Zone Person get 0% on distribution income?

Only if the distribution is carried out in or from a Corporate Tax Designated Zone, goods entering the UAE are imported through that zone, the buyer resells, processes or alters the goods for sale or is a public benefit entity, and every other QFZP condition is met.

Full guideFree Zone or Designated Zone? Your Address Has to Pass Four Different Tax Tests →
What happens if a Qualifying Free Zone Person fails a condition?

It stops being a Qualifying Free Zone Person from the start of that tax period and for the next four tax periods.

Full guideFree Zone or Designated Zone? Your Address Has to Pass Four Different Tax Tests →
Do UAE family foundations pay Corporate Tax?

A family foundation that is a juridical person pays Corporate Tax at 0% up to AED 375,000 of Taxable Income and 9% above, unless the FTA approves it as a fiscally transparent Unincorporated Partnership.

Full guideFamily Foundations and UAE Corporate Tax: Taxed, or Transparent? →
Can a family foundation be tax transparent in the UAE?

Yes, if it benefits identified individuals or a public benefit entity, mainly holds and invests assets, conducts no business, isn't mainly for tax avoidance, and the FTA approves its application before the end of the Tax Period.

Full guideFamily Foundations and UAE Corporate Tax: Taxed, or Transparent? →
Does a transparent family foundation still register and file?

It still registers on EmaraTax. It doesn't file a Tax Return, but it must file an annual confirmation within 9 months of the end of each Tax Period.

Full guideFamily Foundations and UAE Corporate Tax: Taxed, or Transparent? →
Do beneficiaries of a family foundation need to register for Corporate Tax?

Individual beneficiaries only need to register if their own UAE business turnover is over AED 1 million in a calendar year. Beneficiaries that are Taxable Persons, such as a public benefit entity, register and report their share.

Full guideFamily Foundations and UAE Corporate Tax: Taxed, or Transparent? →
Is every company in a UAE free zone taxed at 0%?

No. Only a Qualifying Free Zone Person that meets every condition gets 0%, and only on its Qualifying Income. Other taxable income is taxed at 9%, and a free zone company that doesn't qualify is taxed at the standard rates.

Full guideIs Your Free Zone Company a Qualifying Free Zone Person? The Conditions, Activities and Traps →
What is the de minimis rule for Qualifying Free Zone Persons?

Non-qualifying revenue in a tax period must not exceed 5% of total revenue or AED 5 million, whichever is lower. Breaching it costs QFZP status for that period and the next four.

Full guideIs Your Free Zone Company a Qualifying Free Zone Person? The Conditions, Activities and Traps →
Can a Qualifying Free Zone Person sell to individuals?

Dealings with individuals are an excluded activity under Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025, with narrow exceptions for certain ship, fund management, wealth management and aircraft activities. Income from them counts as non-qualifying revenue.

Full guideIs Your Free Zone Company a Qualifying Free Zone Person? The Conditions, Activities and Traps →
Does a Qualifying Free Zone Person need audited financial statements?

Yes. Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025 requires every Qualifying Free Zone Person to prepare audited financial statements in accordance with Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025, whatever its revenue.

Full guideIs Your Free Zone Company a Qualifying Free Zone Person? The Conditions, Activities and Traps →
Is my free zone a qualifying free zone?

The test is on the company, not the zone. The zone must be a Free Zone for Corporate Tax purposes, which your free zone authority can confirm, and the company must then meet the QFZP conditions itself.

Full guideIs Your Free Zone Company a Qualifying Free Zone Person? The Conditions, Activities and Traps →

Are intra-group invoices excluded from UAE e-invoicing?

Only between members of the same VAT Tax Group, and only until 31 December 2028. Transactions within a Corporate Tax Group that isn't a VAT group are in scope.

Full guideFive Changes to the UAE E-Invoicing Rules, and the One Most Groups Will Misread →
How long do I have to issue a UAE e-invoice?

Within 14 business days of the transaction, subject to the VAT Law's own timing rules.

Full guideFive Changes to the UAE E-Invoicing Rules, and the One Most Groups Will Misread →
Do e-invoices have to be stored in the UAE?

Decision No. 197 only requires you to keep them for the Tax Procedures Law period and produce them on request. It says nothing about location.

Full guideFive Changes to the UAE E-Invoicing Rules, and the One Most Groups Will Misread →
Does UAE e-invoicing apply if I'm not VAT-registered?

Yes. It applies regardless of VAT registration. A business without a TRN needs a 10-digit TIN, generated through EmaraTax.

Full guideDoes UAE E-Invoicing Apply to Your Business? Scope, Exclusions and Your Go-Live Date →
When does UAE e-invoicing start for my business?

Revenue of AED 50 million or more: go live by 1 January 2027. Under AED 50 million: appoint an ASP by 31 March 2027 and go live by 1 July 2027. Government entities: 1 October 2027.

Full guideDoes UAE E-Invoicing Apply to Your Business? Scope, Exclusions and Your Go-Live Date →
Are sales to consumers covered by UAE e-invoicing?

No. B2C supplies are excluded. You keep issuing normal VAT invoices or receipts to consumers.

Full guideDoes UAE E-Invoicing Apply to Your Business? Scope, Exclusions and Your Go-Live Date →
Is a PDF still a valid invoice under UAE e-invoicing?

No. The legal e-invoice is the PINT-AE XML sent through your ASP. A PDF can go alongside it as a copy, but it doesn't replace it.

Full guideHow a UAE E-Invoice Actually Travels: The Five-Corner Model in Plain English →
What is my Peppol Participant Identifier?

0235 followed by your 10-digit TIN, which is the first 10 digits of your TRN.

Full guideHow a UAE E-Invoice Actually Travels: The Five-Corner Model in Plain English →
What if my customer isn't live on e-invoicing yet?

You still issue the e-invoice through your ASP to the placeholder endpoint 0235:9900000098, and also send a normal tax invoice such as a PDF.

Full guideHow a UAE E-Invoice Actually Travels: The Five-Corner Model in Plain English →
Can I use different e-invoicing providers for sales and purchases?

No. You appoint a single ASP for both sending and receiving. Members of a VAT Tax Group can use different ASPs.

Full guideChoosing an E-Invoicing Provider (ASP) in the UAE: What to Check Before You Sign →
What should I check before signing with an ASP?

Accreditation, experience, whether the platform is its own, your ERP connector, security and data location, support and uptime, total cost, and contract terms for archive export and switching.

Full guideChoosing an E-Invoicing Provider (ASP) in the UAE: What to Check Before You Sign →
Who is responsible for keeping e-invoice records?

You are. You can hand storage to the ASP, but the obligation to keep the data for the Tax Procedures Law period, generally 5 years, stays with you.

Full guideChoosing an E-Invoicing Provider (ASP) in the UAE: What to Check Before You Sign →
What is the penalty for not appointing an e-invoicing ASP?

It counts as failing to implement: AED 5,000 for the first instance, then AED 5,000 for each month it continues, capped at AED 50,000 in any 12 months.

Full guideThe New UAE E-Invoicing Penalties: Cabinet Decision No. 173 of 2026 Explained →
What is the penalty for not issuing an e-invoice?

AED 100 per e-invoice or credit note, capped at AED 5,000 per calendar month.

Full guideThe New UAE E-Invoicing Penalties: Cabinet Decision No. 173 of 2026 Explained →
Is voluntary early e-invoicing penalised?

No. Mistakes made before your mandatory date don't attract these penalties.

Full guideThe New UAE E-Invoicing Penalties: Cabinet Decision No. 173 of 2026 Explained →
When does UAE e-invoicing start for large businesses?

Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more in their latest financial statements must go live by 1 January 2027. The deadline to appoint an ASP was 30 October 2026.

Full guideE-Invoicing Goes Live on 1 January: A Six-Week Readiness Plan for AED 50m+ Businesses →
What happens if my business isn't live on 1 January 2027?

Not implementing e-invoicing, including not appointing an ASP, is penalised at AED 5,000 for the first instance and AED 5,000 for each month it continues, capped at AED 50,000 in any 12 months.

Full guideE-Invoicing Goes Live on 1 January: A Six-Week Readiness Plan for AED 50m+ Businesses →
Can I still send PDF invoices after go-live?

The legal invoice is the e-invoice sent through your ASP. A PDF can go alongside it, and to customers who aren't live yet you must also send a normal tax invoice so they can recover input VAT.

Full guideE-Invoicing Goes Live on 1 January: A Six-Week Readiness Plan for AED 50m+ Businesses →
How long do I have to issue an e-invoice?

E-invoices and e-credit notes must be issued and transmitted within 14 business days of the transaction, subject to the VAT Law's own timing rules.

Why does a statutory auditor look at VAT?

VAT affects the balance sheet, errors in VAT usually mean errors in revenue or expenses, and unreported mistakes may need a provision for penalties.

Full guideWhat Your Auditor Actually Tests in Your VAT Returns →
What evidence supports zero-rated exports?

Official and commercial evidence of export within 90 days, such as customs exit documents and bills of lading or airway bills.

Full guideWhat Your Auditor Actually Tests in Your VAT Returns →
What should I prepare before a VAT-related audit?

The returns with a ledger reconciliation, a control account roll-forward agreed to EmaraTax, an export evidence file, a list of blocked input VAT, and the year's FTA correspondence.

Full guideWhat Your Auditor Actually Tests in Your VAT Returns →
Why would the FTA ask about my VAT returns if I did nothing wrong?

If one of your customers or suppliers is investigated for evasion, the FTA can ask every business in that chain for its side of the transactions.

Full guideThe FTA Asked About Our Client's Sales Because a Customer Evaded Tax →
What should I keep for each sale?

The tax invoice and credit notes, delivery proof, the matched payment, the customer's licence and verified TRN, and a sales listing that ties to each return.

Full guideThe FTA Asked About Our Client's Sales Because a Customer Evaded Tax →
What if I find an error while answering an FTA request?

Correct it. Errors over AED 10,000 of tax need a voluntary disclosure.

Full guideThe FTA Asked About Our Client's Sales Because a Customer Evaded Tax →
Why would my bank compare VAT returns with bank credits?

To check that the sales in your VAT returns are consistent with the money coming into your account before it renews or extends a facility.

Full guideThe Bank Compared Our Client's VAT Returns With Its Bank Credits. They Didn't Match. →
What explains a gap between bank credits and VAT sales?

Loans and capital, transfers between your own accounts, refunds and other non-sales receipts, timing, and zero-rated or exempt sales. Whatever is left may be unreported sales.

Full guideThe Bank Compared Our Client's VAT Returns With Its Bank Credits. They Didn't Match. →
How do I correct unreported sales found in the reconciliation?

Over AED 10,000 of tax: file a voluntary disclosure within 20 business days of becoming aware. AED 10,000 or less: correct it in the return for the period you found it.
