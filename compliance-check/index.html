The thresholds it uses

ObligationThreshold
VAT registrationMandatory over AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and imports; voluntary over AED 187,500 of supplies or expenses
Corporate Tax registrationEvery company; individuals with business turnover over AED 1 million
Small Business ReliefRevenue of AED 3 million or less in the period and every earlier period; tax periods ending by 31 December 2029
Audited financial statements (Corporate Tax)Annual turnover over AED 50 million, every Qualifying Free Zone Person and every Tax Group
Transfer Pricing Disclosure FormRelated-party transactions over AED 40 million, or over AED 500,000 to one connected person
Master File and Local FileRevenue of AED 200 million or more, or group revenue of AED 3.15 billion or more
Domestic Minimum Top-up TaxMultinational groups with revenue of EUR 750 million or more in 2 of the 4 preceding years
E-invoicingPhase 1 from 1 January 2027 at AED 50 million or more of revenue; Phase 2 from 1 July 2027
