Supplier Verification Policy for input VAT

Article 5(4) of FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026 expects a written policy naming who runs, reviews and supervises your supplier checks. Most SMEs don't have one. This template gives you a ready-to-edit policy in about 30 minutes.

What's inside

  • Roles and powers for the implementer, reviewer and supervisor, as Article 5(4) requires
  • When to verify: first dealing, each supply, every 12 months, and spend monitoring
  • The spend tiers: AED 10,000, 100,000 and 375,000
  • Supplier, supply and enhanced checks, mapped to Articles 3 and 4
  • Red-flag escalation and payment controls based on the FTA's own examples
  • Records and annual review, plus a one-page Supplier Verification Record to print and sign

4 pages, editable in Word or Google Docs. The fields to fill in are highlighted.
