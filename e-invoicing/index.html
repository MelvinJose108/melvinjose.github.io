E-invoicing means issuing and receiving invoices as structured electronic data instead of PDFs or paper. In the UAE, businesses exchange them through Accredited Service Providers approved by the Ministry of Finance.

A short history

  1. October 2024Legal amendments introduce e-invoicing concepts into UAE law.
  2. February 2026The Ministry of Finance publishes the first e-invoicing guidelines.
  3. 1 July 2026A voluntary pilot phase begins.
  4. 1 January 2027E-invoicing becomes mandatory for businesses with annual revenue of AED 50 million or more.
  5. 1 July and 1 October 2027Mandatory for other in-scope businesses, and then for government entities.

These are the dates reported at the time of writing, and some deadlines have already been adjusted. Check the latest Ministry of Finance announcements.
