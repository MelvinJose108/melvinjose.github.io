Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax on most goods and services in the UAE. Businesses collect it from customers and pass it to the Federal Tax Authority (FTA), after deducting the VAT they paid on their own purchases.

A short history

  1. 2017The UAE issues its federal VAT law, as part of a framework agreed across the Gulf Cooperation Council.
  2. 1 January 2018VAT begins at a standard rate of 5%.
  3. TodayThe standard rate is 5%. Some supplies are zero-rated or exempt, and businesses must register once their taxable supplies pass the threshold.
