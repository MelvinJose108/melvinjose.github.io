Zero-rated supplies (such as exports) carry 0% VAT but still count as taxable supplies. Exempt supplies (such as residential rent or some financial services) carry no VAT, and the input VAT on them generally can't be recovered.

Blocked items include client entertainment, cars available for personal use and personal benefits for staff (Executive Regulation Art. 53). Overheads and reverse-charge VAT are recovered in the ratio of taxable to total sales (the standard method), but the FTA may approve a special method. An estimate only.

It applies the 0%/9% rates, 50% of client entertainment, the 75% cap on loss relief, the interest limit (the higher of AED 12m or 30% of EBITDA), Small Business Relief (revenue up to AED 3m) and the QFZP de minimis test (lower of 5% of revenue or AED 5m). It doesn't cover transfer pricing, Tax Groups, Pillar Two top-up tax or other reliefs. See the Corporate Tax guides.
