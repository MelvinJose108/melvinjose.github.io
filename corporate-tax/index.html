Corporate Tax is a tax on the profits of UAE companies and certain other businesses. It starts from the accounting profit in the financial statements, adjusted for items the law treats differently.

A short history

  1. 31 January 2022The Ministry of Finance announces that the UAE will introduce a federal corporate tax.
  2. December 2022The federal Corporate Tax law is issued.
  3. 1 June 2023The law takes effect for financial years starting on or after that date. Taxable income up to AED 375,000 is taxed at 0%, and the portion above that at 9%.
  4. Since thenCabinet and Ministerial decisions have added detail on free zones, small business relief and filing.
