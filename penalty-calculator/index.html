Penalty Estimator

Estimate FTA administrative penalties for VAT and Corporate Tax under the current rules, with the legal basis for every line. For VAT and Corporate Tax amounts, use the tax calculator.

    Legal basis for each line

      An estimate only, not advice. VAT penalties use Cabinet Decision No. 40 of 2017 as amended by Cabinet Decision No. 129 of 2025 (in force from 14 April 2026). Corporate Tax penalties use Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023 as amended by Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2024. Monthly penalties count each month or part of a month from the day after the due date. Business-day deadlines count from the day after the trigger date and skip weekends and UAE public holidays (built in for 2026 and 2027; Islamic holiday dates for 2027 are expected). The FTA's assessment in EmaraTax is final.

      The main penalties at a glance

      ViolationVAT (from 14 April 2026)Corporate Tax
      Late payment14% a year, charged monthly on the unpaid tax14% a year, charged monthly on the unpaid tax
      Late tax returnAED 1,000; AED 2,000 if repeated within 24 monthsAED 500 a month for 12 months, then AED 1,000 a month
      Voluntary disclosure1% of the tax difference a month, until disclosed1% of the tax difference a month, until disclosed
      Error found by the FTA15% of the tax difference, plus 1% a month15% of the tax difference, plus 1% a month
      Incorrect returnAED 500 (unless corrected in time)AED 500 (unless corrected in time)
      Late registrationAED 10,000AED 10,000 (waiver available in some cases)
      Records not keptAED 10,000; AED 20,000 if repeatedAED 10,000; AED 20,000 if repeated

      The biggest change in 2026 is to VAT late payment. The old 2% immediately plus 4% a month, up to 300%, became a single 14% a year. VAT is now aligned with the Corporate Tax rules.
