Is Your Free Zone Company a Qualifying Free Zone Person? The Conditions, Activities and Traps

The six conditions to be a Qualifying Free Zone Person and get 0% UAE Corporate Tax, the qualifying and excluded activities under Ministerial Decision 229 of 2025, the de minimis test, and the five-year cost of failing.

By Melvin Jose · 21 November 2026 · Last reviewed 21 November 2026 · 5 min read

Based on Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025 · issued 28 August 2025, effective from 1 June 2023

In short

A free zone company only pays 0% Corporate Tax if it is a Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP): it must keep adequate substance in the free zone, earn Qualifying Income, keep non-qualifying revenue within the lower of 5% of total revenue or AED 5 million, not elect the standard rates, follow transfer pricing rules, and prepare audited financial statements. The 0% covers Qualifying Income only. Failing any condition removes QFZP status from the start of that tax period and for the next four.

“We’re in a free zone, so we pay 0%.” It’s the most common Corporate Tax assumption I see, and it’s wrong more often than people expect.

The 0% rate belongs to a Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP), not to the licence or the zone. A company has to earn it by meeting every condition, in every tax period. And the 0% only applies to Qualifying Income. Anything else it earns is taxed at 9%.

First: it’s the company that qualifies, not the zone

People search for “is my free zone a qualifying free zone”. There’s no such category. Two separate things have to be true:

  1. The zone must be a Free Zone for Corporate Tax purposes. Your free zone authority can confirm it. (The four zone tests)
  2. The company must meet the QFZP conditions below.

The six conditions

  1. Adequate substance in the free zone. Its core income-generating activities happen in the free zone, with enough qualified full-time staff, assets and operating spending for the business. A registered address with no people doesn’t pass.
  2. It earns Qualifying Income. Broadly, income from transactions with other Free Zone Persons, and from qualifying activities with anyone else, as long as the activity isn’t an excluded one.
  3. Non-qualifying revenue stays within de minimis. It must not exceed 5% of total revenue or AED 5 million, whichever is lower.
  4. It hasn’t elected the standard rates. Opting into the normal 9% regime gives up QFZP status.
  5. It follows transfer pricing rules. Related-party transactions at arm’s length, with a Master File and Local File where the thresholds require them.
  6. It prepares audited financial statements. Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025 requires them for every QFZP, in line with Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025, regardless of revenue.

Which activities qualify, and which never do

Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025 replaced the 2023 list in September 2025, with effect from 1 June 2023.

Qualifying activities:

  • manufacturing of goods or materials, and processing of goods or materials
  • trading of qualifying commodities
  • holding shares and other securities for investment
  • ownership, management and operation of ships
  • reinsurance, fund management, and wealth and investment management services
  • headquarter services to related parties
  • treasury and financing services to related parties, or for its own account
  • financing and leasing of aircraft
  • distribution of goods or materials in or from a Designated Zone
  • logistics services
  • activities ancillary to any of the above

Excluded activities:

  • dealings with individuals, except certain ship, fund management, wealth management and aircraft activities
  • banking
  • insurance, except where it falls within reinsurance or headquarter services
  • finance and leasing, except the qualifying ones above
  • owning or operating immovable property, except commercial property in a free zone transacted with Free Zone Persons
  • activities ancillary to these

The traps that cost the 0%

Selling to individuals. A free zone trader whose customers include walk-in or online retail buyers is dealing with individuals. That revenue is non-qualifying, and it counts against the de minimis limit.

Distribution outside a Designated Zone. Distribution only qualifies if it runs in or from a zone that is a Designated Zone for Corporate Tax, the goods are imported through that zone, and the buyer resells or processes them. (Distribution conditions)

Commodity trading by a logistics-heavy business. Trading of qualifying commodities doesn’t qualify if 51% or more of the company’s revenue in the tax period comes from distribution, warehousing, logistics or inventory management.

Breaching de minimis by a small amount. On AED 40 million of revenue, the limit is AED 2 million (5%). On AED 200 million, it’s AED 5 million (the cap). One unusual contract can tip a company over.

Skipping the audit. No audited financial statements, no QFZP status, however clean the activities are.

The cost of failing: five years, not one

If a QFZP fails any condition at any point in a tax period, it stops being a QFZP from the start of that tax period and for the next four. That’s five tax periods at the standard rates.

A rough sense of scale: a free zone company with AED 10 million of taxable income pays nothing as a QFZP. Taxed at the standard rates, the tax is 9% on everything above AED 375,000, about AED 866,000 a year, or over AED 4.3 million across the five periods.

What to do now

  1. Classify every revenue stream as qualifying, excluded or other, and record why. Check customer types, especially individuals.
  2. Calculate de minimis each quarter, not just at year end, so you see a breach coming.
  3. Document substance: who does the core work, where they sit, and what assets and spending are in the free zone.
  4. Check the zone, and if you distribute goods, whether it’s a Designated Zone for Corporate Tax.
  5. Book the audit, and make sure your transfer pricing files are in order.

Common questions

Is every company in a UAE free zone taxed at 0%?

No. Only a Qualifying Free Zone Person that meets every condition gets 0%, and only on its Qualifying Income. Other taxable income is taxed at 9%, and a free zone company that doesn't qualify is taxed at the standard rates.

What is the de minimis rule for Qualifying Free Zone Persons?

Non-qualifying revenue in a tax period must not exceed 5% of total revenue or AED 5 million, whichever is lower. Breaching it costs QFZP status for that period and the next four.

Can a Qualifying Free Zone Person sell to individuals?

Dealings with individuals are an excluded activity under Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025, with narrow exceptions for certain ship, fund management, wealth management and aircraft activities. Income from them counts as non-qualifying revenue.

Does a Qualifying Free Zone Person need audited financial statements?

Yes. Ministerial Decision No. 229 of 2025 requires every Qualifying Free Zone Person to prepare audited financial statements in accordance with Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025, whatever its revenue.

Is my free zone a qualifying free zone?

The test is on the company, not the zone. The zone must be a Free Zone for Corporate Tax purposes, which your free zone authority can confirm, and the company must then meet the QFZP conditions itself.
