Family Foundations and UAE Corporate Tax: Taxed, or Transparent?

How UAE Corporate Tax treats a family foundation: when it is taxed at 9%, the five conditions to be treated as transparent, registration, returns and what beneficiaries must do.

By Melvin Jose · 20 November 2026 · Last reviewed 20 November 2026 · 5 min read

Based on FTA Basic Tax Information Bulletin, Treatment of a Family Foundation · October 2026

In short

A UAE family foundation that is a juridical person is taxed like a company (0% up to AED 375,000, then 9%) unless it meets five conditions and the FTA approves an application to treat it as a fiscally transparent Unincorporated Partnership. Then the foundation isn't taxed itself, files only an annual confirmation, and its beneficiaries are taxed on their share if they are Taxable Persons. Either way, the foundation must register for Corporate Tax.

Families are increasingly holding wealth, property and investments through foundations and trusts in the UAE. Corporate Tax gives these structures a choice: be taxed like a company, or be treated as transparent so the foundation itself pays nothing.

The FTA’s Basic Tax Information Bulletin on the Treatment of a Family Foundation (October 2026) sets out how that choice works. Here is what it means in practice.

What counts as a family foundation

For Corporate Tax, a Family Foundation is any foundation, trust or similar entity set up to hold and manage family wealth for its beneficiaries, and that meets the conditions below.

One exception matters: a company such as an LLC is not “similar to a foundation or trust”, so it can’t be a Family Foundation, unless it is wholly owned and controlled by one.

Route 1: taxed like a company (the default)

A family foundation that is a juridical person is, by default, a Taxable Person in its own right. It:

  • registers on EmaraTax as a Taxable Person,
  • pays Corporate Tax at 0% on Taxable Income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above, or, if it is a Qualifying Free Zone Person, 0% on Qualifying Income and 9% on the rest, and
  • files a Tax Return within 9 months of the end of each Tax Period.

Route 2: transparent, with FTA approval

A transparent foundation isn’t taxed itself. It’s treated as an Unincorporated Partnership, and its members are taxed on their share of its assets, liabilities, income and expenses.

A foundation that is not a juridical person is transparent already, and registers on EmaraTax as an Unincorporated Partnership. A foundation that is a juridical person has to qualify and apply.

The five conditions to be transparent

Under Ministerial Decision No. 261 of 2024 and FTA Decision No. 5 of 2025, a family foundation that is a juridical person is treated as transparent only if all five are met:

  1. Beneficiaries: it was set up for identified or identifiable individuals, a public benefit entity, or both.
  2. Activity: its main activity is receiving, holding, investing, disbursing or disposing of savings and investment assets, or otherwise managing them.
  3. No business: it does nothing that would be a Business if the founder, settlor or a beneficiary did it directly or held the assets themselves.
  4. Purpose: avoiding Corporate Tax is not its main or principal purpose.
  5. Approval: it applies to the FTA to be treated as an Unincorporated Partnership, and the FTA approves.

Miss any one, or don’t get approval, and the foundation stays taxable.

The timing matters. The application must be made before the end of the Tax Period it applies to. It can take effect from the current Tax Period, the next one, or another date the FTA sets.

Condition 3 is the one to test hardest. The question isn’t what the foundation calls itself, but whether the same activity, done by the founder or a beneficiary personally, would count as a Business for Corporate Tax. A foundation running a trading operation, for example, would fail it.

Registration and filing, side by side

  Taxed (opaque) Transparent
Register on EmaraTax Yes, as a Taxable Person Yes, as an Unincorporated Partnership (or registered, then approved)
Tax Return Within 9 months of year end Not required
Annual confirmation Not applicable Within 9 months of year end
Who pays tax The foundation Beneficiaries that are Taxable Persons, on their share

Every family foundation formed under UAE law has to register, one way or the other. A juridical-person foundation registers as a Taxable Person first, and can then apply for transparency.

What beneficiaries need to do

  • Individual beneficiaries only register and file if they run a business in the UAE outside the foundation and its turnover is over AED 1 million in a calendar year.
  • Beneficiaries that are Taxable Persons, such as a public benefit entity, register in their own right and include their share of a transparent foundation’s results in their own Tax Return.

Penalties

Missing a registration, return or annual confirmation can attract administrative penalties under the Corporate Tax penalty rules. The Penalty Estimator estimates Corporate Tax penalties for late filing and late payment.

What to do now

  1. Check the registration. Make sure the foundation is registered on EmaraTax, as a Taxable Person or an Unincorporated Partnership.
  2. Test the five conditions honestly, especially condition 3. Write down why each one is met.
  3. If transparency fits, apply before the Tax Period ends. After that, the foundation is taxable for that period.
  4. Diarise the 9-month deadline, for the Tax Return or the annual confirmation. The Deadline Planner works it out from the year end.
  5. Tell individual beneficiaries where they stand. Most won’t need to register, but those with their own UAE businesses over AED 1 million will.

Source: FTA Basic Tax Information Bulletin, Treatment of a Family Foundation (October 2026), referring to Ministerial Decision No. 261 of 2024 and FTA Decision No. 5 of 2025. For the full rules, see the FTA’s Corporate Tax Guide on the Taxation of Family Foundations.

Common questions

Do UAE family foundations pay Corporate Tax?

A family foundation that is a juridical person pays Corporate Tax at 0% up to AED 375,000 of Taxable Income and 9% above, unless the FTA approves it as a fiscally transparent Unincorporated Partnership.

Can a family foundation be tax transparent in the UAE?

Yes, if it benefits identified individuals or a public benefit entity, mainly holds and invests assets, conducts no business, isn't mainly for tax avoidance, and the FTA approves its application before the end of the Tax Period.

Does a transparent family foundation still register and file?

It still registers on EmaraTax. It doesn't file a Tax Return, but it must file an annual confirmation within 9 months of the end of each Tax Period.

Do beneficiaries of a family foundation need to register for Corporate Tax?

Individual beneficiaries only need to register if their own UAE business turnover is over AED 1 million in a calendar year. Beneficiaries that are Taxable Persons, such as a public benefit entity, register and report their share.
