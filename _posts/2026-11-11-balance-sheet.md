<p>A balance sheet is a snapshot of what a business owns and owes on one date. It always balances: assets equal liabilities plus equity.</p>
<h3>Three quick checks</h3><ul><li><b>Liquidity:</b> are current assets larger than current liabilities?</li><li><b>Debt:</b> how much is funded by borrowing compared with owners’ money?</li><li><b>Trends:</b> compare with last year, because the direction matters more than one number.</li></ul>
