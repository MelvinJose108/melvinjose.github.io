<p>An audit is an independent check that financial statements give a fair picture of a business. Most audits follow the same path.</p>
<h3>The stages</h3><ul><li><b>Planning:</b> understand the business, set materiality, and agree timing.</li><li><b>Risk assessment:</b> find where errors are most likely.</li><li><b>Fieldwork:</b> test balances and transactions, and collect evidence.</li><li><b>Reporting:</b> discuss findings with management, then issue the audit opinion.</li></ul>
<p class="tip">The smoother your trial balance and supporting documents are on day one, the shorter the audit.</p>
