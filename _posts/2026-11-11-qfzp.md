title: "Qualifying Free Zone Persons explained"
topic: "Corporate Tax"
description: "How a free zone company can reach a 0% rate on qualifying income, and what can switch it off."
reviewed: 2026-11-11
---
<p>A Free Zone Person that meets the conditions to be a Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) can pay 0% on its qualifying income. Income that does not qualify is taxed at the standard rate.</p>
<h3>The ideas behind the rules</h3><ul><li>The business needs real activity and people in the free zone.</li><li>Its income must come from activities the law treats as qualifying.</li><li>It must keep proper records and meet the other conditions each year.</li></ul>
<p>Failing a condition can mean losing QFZP status for a period, so this is worth reviewing every year, not just at set-up.</p>
<p class="tip">Rules and cabinet decisions on qualifying activities are updated from time to time. Always check the latest version.</p>
