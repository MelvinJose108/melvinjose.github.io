An audit is an independent examination of a company’s financial statements, so that owners, lenders and regulators can rely on them. In the UAE, many companies need an audit by a licensed auditor, whether because of the law, their free zone, a lender or their shareholders.

Why it matters for tax

  1. Financial statementsCorporate Tax starts from the accounting profit in a company’s financial statements.
  2. VAT recordsVAT returns should agree with the books, and auditors often test this.
  3. EvidenceGood records make both audits and tax reviews faster and less costly.
