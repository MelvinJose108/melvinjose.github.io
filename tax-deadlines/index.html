Deadline Planner

Enter your financial year-end and VAT period to see your next Corporate Tax, VAT and e-invoicing deadlines, adjusted for weekends and UAE public holidays. Then add them to your calendar with a 7-day reminder.

    The .ics file works with Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar, and each deadline gets a 7-day reminder. Deadlines that fall on a weekend or public holiday move to the next business day (Tax Procedures Law, Article 49). Public holidays are built in for 2026 and 2027; Islamic holiday dates for 2027 are expected dates. Some first tax periods and FTA-approved extensions have different dates, so check EmaraTax for your own.

    The rules behind the dates

    ObligationDeadline
    Corporate Tax return and paymentWithin 9 months of the end of the tax period. For a 31 December year-end, that's 30 September of the next year.
    VAT return and paymentBy the 28th day after the end of each monthly or quarterly tax period
    E-invoicing (revenue of AED 50m or more)Appoint an ASP by 30 October 2026; go live by 1 January 2027
    E-invoicing (revenue under AED 50m)Appoint an ASP by 31 March 2027; go live by 1 July 2027
    Any deadline on a weekend or public holidayMoves to the next business day
