Supplier Verification

Work out which checks FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026 expects for a supplier, spot the red flags from the FTA's own examples, and save a signed evidence record for your file. New to the rules? Start with the 5-part guide.

1. Supplier and spend

2. Risk indicators

3. Checks done and evidence on file

4. Sign-off

Nothing you enter leaves your browser. To save the record, choose "Save as PDF" in the print window and file it with the supplier's documents. The price flag is this tool's rule of thumb (more than 20% outside the market range), not a figure set by the FTA.

Paste your suppliers from Excel (three columns: name, spend in the past 12 months, expected spend in the next 12 months, excluding VAT) to see which tier each one falls into.

How the tiers work

Spend with the supplierWhat Decision No. 13 expects
A supply under AED 10,000, and not over AED 100,000 with that supplier in the past or next 12 monthsChecks may be skipped for that supply
Everything elseSupplier checks (identity, place of business, risk indicators) and supply checks (reason, payment, price, licence, origin)
Over AED 375,000 with that supplier in the past or next 12 monthsAll of the above, plus written confirmation of its UAE bank account and a review of its public reputation

Re-verify recurring suppliers at least every 12 months, and keep a written policy naming who runs, reviews and supervises the checks. More detail: the three spend thresholds and the evidence file and policy.
