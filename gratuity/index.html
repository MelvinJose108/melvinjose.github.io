Gratuity Calculator

End-of-service gratuity for private-sector employees under Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, plus a year-end provision for your whole team.

    Paste your staff list from Excel: name, start date, basic monthly salary, and (optional) unpaid leave days. The provision is what you'd owe if everyone left on the date below.

    For full-time employees in the private sector, including most free zones (DIFC and ADGM have their own employment rules). Daily wage = basic salary ÷ 30; a year = 365 days; part years are pro-rated; unpaid leave doesn't count as service; the total is capped at two years' basic salary. Gratuity is due only after one year of continuous service, and resignation no longer reduces it. Employers on the voluntary savings scheme calculate differently. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

    How gratuity is calculated

    ServiceGratuity
    Under 1 yearNone
    Years 1 to 521 days' basic salary for each year
    After 5 years30 days' basic salary for each additional year
    MaximumTwo years' basic salary in total

    Worked examples

    Basic salaryServiceWorkingGratuity
    AED 6,0002 yearsAED 200 a day × 21 days × 2 yearsAED 8,400
    AED 10,0003 yearsAED 333.33 a day × 21 days × 3 yearsAED 21,000
    AED 12,0007 yearsAED 400 × 21 × 5 = 42,000, plus AED 400 × 30 × 2 = 24,000AED 66,000

    The employer must pay final dues, including gratuity, within 14 days of the contract ending.

    For the accounts: the year-end provision is usually the gratuity everyone would receive if they left on the balance sheet date. Larger entities reporting under full IFRS may need an actuarial valuation under IAS 19 instead.
