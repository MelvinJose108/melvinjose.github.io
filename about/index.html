Melvin Jose

Senior Audit Executive · Ajman, UAE · Chartered Accountant finalist

I'm a Chartered Accountant finalist with 6+ years in audit and tax across the UAE and India. I lead Corporate Tax, VAT and audit engagements for Free Zone and mainland businesses.

Most of my work sits where tax rules meet day-to-day bookkeeping: a Corporate Tax return that depends on how the year's entries were booked, a VAT refund that stands or falls on the documents behind it, an FTA query that needs a clear, well-supported answer. I write here to make those rules easier to apply before they become a problem.

In numbers

  • 200+ Corporate Tax engagements delivered, including registration, return filing, QFZP assessments and Small Business Relief evaluations
  • AED 3 million+ in VAT refunds supported through documentation and FTA submissions
  • 6+ years in audit and tax across the UAE and India, including 2.5+ years on UAE Corporate Tax and VAT

What I work on

  • UAE Corporate Tax: registration, returns, QFZP assessments, Small Business Relief, and Corporate Tax and transfer pricing reports
  • VAT: registrations, returns, VAT reviews, Tax Groups, deregistration and refund claims
  • FTA matters: clarifications, reconsideration requests, penalty waiver submissions and audit queries
  • Statutory audit and reporting: IFRS-based financial statements, audit planning and risk assessment
  • Sectors: trading, logistics, services, contracting, schools and Free Zone entities
